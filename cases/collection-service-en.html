Collection Service

Debt collection without extra steps. Calling debtors and recording promises to pay. An off-the-shelf system that had to become fast for collection agents.

Role
Product Designer
Format
In-house product
System
Debt collection service
Users
Lead specialist, Skip tracer

Approach

Calling debtors in an off-the-shelf system where the full picture was scattered across tabs. I built a new interface on top of the system’s data and processes.

Decisions

  1. 01
    Put the debtor on one screen
    Debt, contacts, assets and events are visible before the call.
  2. 02
    Prioritized phone numbers
    The agent starts with the number most likely to get through.
  3. 03
    Expanded each asset into its own page
    Payments and promises for every debt in one place.

Result

1 screen
All call prep, without going through the tabs of the off-the-shelf system
3 steps
New contact, reaching the client and a promise to pay in a single flow
2 roles
The lead specialist and the skip tracer work in one interface
